Receiving & Overage Control

The T-556 Overage Hold Tool

A targeted inventory control process designed to identify potential receiving overages, secure affected inventory, and support timely verification before the discrepancy moves further downstream.

Why We Run It

Catch receiving defects while the evidence is still actionable.

Inbound volume makes it impractical to physically audit every receipt at the dock. As a result, some receiving defects are not identified until the inventory has already entered warehouse storage.

Once that happens, determining where the discrepancy originated becomes more difficult. Without timely follow up to the original receipt, a defect discovered during a standard bin audit may appear to be a warehouse inventory defect rather than an inbound receiving defect.

The Overage Hold process closes that gap by identifying potential overages from receiving data and preserving an opportunity to investigate the original receipt before the supporting information loses operational value.

The Process

From flag to resolution

A consistent workflow for identifying, controlling, and resolving potential overages.

  1. Identify

    Review the Splunk report for system-flagged overage receipts.

  2. Prioritize

    Apply the appropriate filters to identify receipts that warrant investigation.

  3. Hold

    For stored inventory, use the PUT location to place the affected location on hold.

  4. Audit

    Physically verify the inventory and determine whether an overage occurred.

  5. Resolve & Release

    Address confirmed discrepancies and release the location when the hold is no longer required.

Why Speed Matters

A hold is only effective when it is resolved promptly.

The tool gives us visibility and a process. What we do with it is on us.

Placing a location on hold is a temporary control, not the completion of the process. While a hold protects potentially discrepant inventory from being withdrawn before verification, it also makes that location unavailable to normal withdrawal activity.

For that reason, every hold should be treated as timesensitive.
ICQA due diligence requires held locations to be investigated accurately and efficiently, allowing confirmed discrepancies to be addressed and valid inventory to be returned to normal availability as quickly as possible.

Efficiency does not mean sacrificing audit quality for speed. The objective is to complete a thorough investigation without allowing unnecessary hold time to create downstream operational impact.

Left too long

Withdrawal exceptions

A location that remains on hold longer than necessary may contain inventory the building needs but cannot access. Timely auditing and release minimize withdrawal exceptions while preserving the inventory control benefit of the process.

More than an inventory audit

Following a flagged receipt through a targeted dock audit provides more actionable data points than discovering the same discrepancy later through a standard bin location audit.

The receipt, PO, DPCI, PID, receiving activity, and storage location can be evaluated as part of the same investigation. This creates a stronger connection between the physical discrepancy and the process that introduced it.

Better traceability improves defect attribution, accelerates reporting, and gives Inbound greater confidence when a defect is identified as originating within the receiving process.

Better data → faster attribution → stronger corrective action → improved building protection and DPMO performance.
